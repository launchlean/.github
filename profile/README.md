## Welcome to LaunchLean

LaunchLean brings practical technology services and new digital products together. We work across hands-on IT support, business research, creative tools, self-hosted media and connected devices. Our aim is simple: make technology easier to use, understand and make your own.

## What we do

| Project | What it does | Current stage |
|---|---|---|
| [LaunchLean](https://launchlean.co.za) | Onsite and remote IT support, business technology services, and a shared account platform | Service platform active; some online request and product workflows are still being connected |
| [Business Discovery](https://launchlean.co.za/discovery/) | Scoped business research requests with credit tracking and human review before research begins | Pilot workflow connected to an internal research service |
| [3D Printing](https://launchlean.co.za/3d-printing/) | Browser-based STL viewing, layout and print estimates, with a marketplace experience | Studio is usable; marketplace orders and payments are in development |

## Built with care

We favor clear service boundaries, useful interfaces and systems that can be operated and understood. Across the platform, that means:

- Keeping customer accounts and service access tied to server-side identity.
- Making research requests wait for human review before work is queued.
- Showing whether a product is available, in pilot or still a preview.
- Building local-first services for media and connected devices.
- Treating customer records, credentials and runtime data as private operational data.
